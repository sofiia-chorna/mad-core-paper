  heatmap) were re-drawn from their committed `data/` folders: **0 differing pixels** against the
  committed PDFs.
- The learning curves (Fig. 9), the SI learning curves by test part and the SI water RDF were
  re-drawn from the dev repo's committed tables with the same matplotlib minor version as the
  originals: **0 differing pixels** against the dev-repo builds. The learning-curve PDF

  drawing cannot be re-run because its crop-window file was deleted (gap G1).

  delta-learning identical; accrete-levels: three of five columns have a source).
- The composition, coverage and element-coverage numbers in the text were recomputed from the
  committed figure data or from the store parquets (341 / 26 / 235 / 340,555 / 94% / 0.752 /
  0.218 / 0.304 / 2,158 / 480) and all agree.

## Recommendation

Build the release as a single modest repository (`05_layout_and_sizes.md`) whose first tier,
about 97 MB, gives verified level-1 reproduction of 11 of the 13 figures and of all four SI
tables and all claim numbers, documents levels 2 and 3 for every workflow without rerunning
them, and references the two Materials Cloud records and the Hugging Face models. Treat the
force map as level-1-blocked until the crop window is rebuilt (one bounded GPU job) and say so
in the guide. Ship the 1.3 GB of trained PET checkpoints as a separate optional Zenodo file,
since nothing else archives them and the water MD cannot be rerun without the two delta
models. Do not ship the 134 MB population file or the 2.7 GB of GFRE descriptors unless offline
level-2 reproduction of Figs. 3, 8 and SI 1 is wanted; both are derivable from the records
and the stored features. Commit and push the scikit-matter sketch-map code and the metatrain
SPACE fix before packaging, otherwise the sketch-map level 3 is irreproducible.
