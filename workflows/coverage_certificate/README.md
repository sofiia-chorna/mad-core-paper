Checks of the separation and coverage guarantee on the released features. Stage A assigns
every pool structure to its nearest centre, stage B closes the lower bound by brute force,
stage C recomputes the separation of every level in float64. levels.json is the result
shipped in the release: for each power of two level the size, the number of MAD-NEXT
members, the separation and the coverage. epsilon_exact recomputes epsilon_sq from the
released features. The large per folder arrays stayed on the development tree.
